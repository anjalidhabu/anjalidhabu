> Hi! I’m Dr. Anjali Dhabu, a civil engineer and researcher working at the intersection of seismology, earthquake engineering, and structural dynamics. I study how the ground moves during earthquakes and how these motions affect buildings and infrastructure. My research combines analytical models, numerical simulations, and seismic measurements to understand translational and rotational ground motions, with applications in earthquake-resistant design and structural health monitoring.
I completed my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras. Currently I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University and principal investigator for the  DFG-funded HERS project, investigating how heterogeneities in Earth’s subsurface influence rotational seismic motion.  I routinely utilize supercomputers for simulations combine them with data-driven techniques and theoretical analysis to uncover the physical mechanisms of seismic shaking.
